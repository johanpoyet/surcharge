Attached: recorded on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS, with the submitted build (TestFlight). It starts from launch and shows account creation, onboarding, a typical workout, logout/login and account deletion (Profil > Supprimer mon compte).

2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Surcharge is a strength training logbook for people who lift weights at the gym (15+). Problem: tracking sets on paper or in notes is slow and makes progress hard to see. Value: users log each set (weight, reps, perceived difficulty) in a few taps, even offline, get a rest timer and a load suggestion based on their last session, plan their week, and see records and progression charts. The app is free, with no ads, no subscription and no in-app purchase.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
Demo account (10 weeks of history): demo.surcharge@johanpoyet.fr / password in the Sign-In Information fields.
- Home (Accueil): today's planned workout, monthly stats, regularity calendar.
- Start a workout: "+" button or Sessions tab (Séances) > a workout > Start. Log sets with +/- buttons, rate difficulty, rest timer starts automatically, finish to see the summary.
- Planning: Sessions tab > Planning (week/month).
- Exercises tab: library, exercise detail with records and progression chart.
- Profile (Profil): body weight, records, settings, CSV export, account deletion.
New users can also sign up with email, Sign in with Apple or Google. No sample files are needed.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Supabase (hosted in the EU, Paris): authentication, database sync and private photo storage.
- Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In: optional login methods.
- Resend (EU): password reset emails only.
- Sentry (EU): anonymous crash reports (no user identifier, no IP).
No payment processor, no AI service, no advertising or analytics SDK.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app is in French and works identically in all regions where it is available.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. Surcharge is not a medical device and gives no medical advice; body weight is optional personal tracking. All content is created by the user and stays private to their account (nothing is shared between users), and the app contains no protected third-party material.
